Apple M2 · public corpora, not real calls · How we measured

Apple M2, 24 GB, macOS 27, release build. Public corpora (AMI meetings, VoxConverse, Spanish parliament speech and audiobooks) built to resemble one side of a call — not real calls. Speaker figures score the remote speakers’ speech with a 0.25 s collar; settings were tuned on 18 other recordings (9.4% there). Method and limits →
