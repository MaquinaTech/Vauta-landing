Vauta listens to any call, transcribes it on your Mac and turns it into minutes, decisions and tasks you can search and ask about. No bot. No cloud. No account. Audio is never saved.

01

It hears what
your Mac hears.

Your microphone comes in as Me, the call as Others: Zoom, Teams, Slack, FaceTime, Webex, or Meet in Safari, Chrome or Firefox. In a room, everything your Mac hears is Me. No bot joins, nobody else installs anything, and Vauta hands you a ready-to-paste notice to tell them.

Me Others

Verified in real calls so far: Safari. All system audio covers the rest.

02

It writes it down.
On your Mac.

Apple’s speech recognizer transcribes both sides in memory, two small bundled models tell the other participants apart, and the model you choose writes the minutes: summary, decisions, tasks with owners and dates, open questions.

No quote, no item. Every decision and task must quote the transcript word for word — and a name, date or figure that doesn’t match is marked Unconfirmed.

03

Then it locks
it away.

Audio is never written to disk. Transcripts and minutes are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, the key lives in your Keychain, and the Meetings window opens with Touch ID or your password. Nothing goes to a Vauta cloud — there isn’t one.

  • Audio transcribed in memory, then discarded
  • Encrypted store, key in your Keychain
  • No telemetry, analytics, crash reports or account
  • Transcripts deleted after 90 days; minutes kept as long as you choose

Ask, with citations

Ask across every meeting. Each answer cites the moment it came from and one click opens the transcript right there — and an answer whose quotes can’t be found says Not verified.

Who said what

The other participants become Speaker 1, Speaker 2… on your Mac. Name them once and the names flow into tasks, search and exports: Markdown, PDF, SRT, WebVTT, JSON.

Shorthand in, full notes out

Type “beta thu, small teams first”. Vauta writes the full note from what was said, every sentence cited, your words always first.

A brief before you join

With Calendar on, by default ten minutes before a meeting: who is coming, what the last one left open, related meetings and documents.

Follow up, your way

Open tasks become Reminders, the next meeting a Calendar event. Nothing is written until you press Add — and Undo takes it back.

Your documents, too

PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Markdown, images… in an encrypted index that never changes your files, so Ask answers from them with a verified quote.

Your model, your rules

Start with Apple Intelligence on this Mac: no setup, basic quality. On a Mac with 16 GB or more, the optional integrated Gemma 4 model reads a whole meeting at once on your GPU. Or use your own server — LM Studio, Ollama, llama.cpp, MLX, vLLM — on this Mac or your local network: bigger models, better minutes. Vauta refuses any model server on the internet.

When you start recording, Vauta offers a short notice in the meeting’s language to paste into the chat, and remembers when you marked everyone as informed. It never sends it for you.

Privacy you can audit

Don’t trust us.
Check.

Network Activity lists every connection Vauta makes — where, when and how much, never the content. Every request goes through one audited code path, and a test fails if any other file opens a connection.

  • Your meetings go only to the model server you choose, on this Mac or your local network — and nowhere at all with Apple Intelligence or the integrated model
  • Never sends your meetings to the internet: no proxy, no redirects. The only internet request is an optional model download you start, and it carries nothing about you
  • Never listens for incoming connections; App Sandbox and hardened runtime
  • Open source: read every line, build it yourself
