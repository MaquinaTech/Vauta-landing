Connected memory

Every meeting and document, connected
Ask once and Vauta searches them all

  • One index for everything

    Transcripts, minutes, notes and your PDF, Word, Excel and PowerPoint files, searched together.

  • Search by meaning, in any language

    With EmbeddingGemma 2 on your Mac, a question in English finds what was said in Spanish. Names, figures and codes still match exactly.

  • People, projects and tasks

    Vauta links the people, projects, clients and topics across your meetings, with one list of open tasks and their owners.

  • Context before each meeting

    Who is coming, what the last meeting with them left open, and related meetings, documents and reminders. Each line opens its source.

  • Suggestions you confirm

    After each meeting, reminders for open tasks and calendar events for agreed follow-ups. Nothing is added until you confirm.

The search model is optional: 574 MB, downloaded only when you choose. Without it, Ask searches by keywords.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does Vauta send my meetings to the cloud?

No. Vauta transcribes on your Mac with Apple’s speech recognition. The minutes are written by Apple Intelligence on your Mac, or by a model server you run on your Mac or your local network; servers on the internet are refused. The internet is used only to download a model, when you start the download.

Does a bot join my calls?

No. Vauta captures the audio on your Mac: your microphone as Me and the call audio as Others. It offers a notice to paste in the chat so participants know they are being transcribed.

Which Macs and call apps does it work with?

Macs with Apple silicon and macOS 26 or later. It captures any call app on the Mac, such as Zoom, Teams, Meet, FaceTime, Slack or Webex; so far it has been tested in real calls in Safari.

Is the audio recorded?

No. The audio is transcribed in memory and discarded. Transcripts and minutes are stored encrypted (AES-256) with the key in your Keychain, and transcripts are deleted after 90 days.

Which languages does it support?

Every language Apple’s speech recognition supports on your macOS. Minutes are written in the meeting’s language or yours, and live subtitles can translate the other participants into your language.

Is it free? Do I need an account?

Free and open source (Apache-2.0), with no account and no telemetry. You build it on your Mac from the source code with Apple’s Command Line Tools; Xcode and an Apple account are not needed. Version 0.9 is a public beta.
